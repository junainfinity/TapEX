# Lynx probability preview adaptation

The local EWMA variance, normalized price/time coordinates and multilinear table interpolation are adapted from `lynx-gameroom-client` version `0.0.1-alpha.1`, Copyright (c) 2026 Lynx Protocol, under the included MIT license (`LICENSE-LYNX`). Its inspected ESM SHA-256 is `5d4059391dfdf3115e7aa4bf69ac8c04dddfb4cfb326603993595cf00c03e264`.

The published touch table failed Maya's sampled-in-band event compatibility check: it assigns positive probability to a zero-width band, while a discrete Gaussian observation has zero probability of landing at one exact point. Published-table interpolation remains for forensic comparison and its domain remains a conservative availability restriction; **runtime odds are not table-priced or provider market-maker quotes**.

New quotes use Maya's `maya-gaussian-linear-six-v2` model: deterministic Gauss-Legendre quadrature evaluates one minus the probability that all six Gaussian observations are below the band and minus the probability that all six are above. This matches band entry by the straight connectors over the five-second window. The forecast uses observed log-return EWMA (lambda .94), source-time anchor, public house edge and minimum gross multiplier. New paper bets and the chart share immutable first-persisted observations. Historical v1 positions retain their original `maya-gaussian-discrete-five-v1` quote and sampled settlement rule. There is no volatility floor, generated live price path or random win/loss in the application. Probability is a local indicative model assumption, not empirically calibrated market odds. The developer-only independent seeded path reference is outside the application runtime.

Maya explicitly validates table domain instead of silently clamping and only records local paper quotes. It does not call the provider's trade, authentication, wallet or funding methods. The public table is fetched from the Lynx-published URL; the SDK software license is not a claim of a white-label license to the provider service.
