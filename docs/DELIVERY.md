## Delivered experience

Maya offers BTC/USD and ETH/USD, live Lynx USD observations, simulated paper quotes, a Three.js price/time board and adaptive desktop/tablet/mobile controls. Stakes are $0.10/$0.50/$1/$5/$10; zoom is continuous from 0.345 to 0.9, initially 0.6. Squares are five seconds wide and $10 BTC/$0.50 ETH high. Paper funds start at $1,000 and can be refilled. The total stake cap is $50 per canonical square.

New v2 bets win when the recorded price line touches the accepted square. First-persisted one-second observations form an immutable paper-game tape; straight connectors and exact rational collision tests count crossings between adjacent observations. Both price edges count; end time does not. The shown multiplier includes the stake. Arithmetic and journal reconciliation use exact decimal/integer values. Complete losses require six observations over the five-second window. Missing evidence waits for recovery and can refund. Legacy v1 bets retain their original sampled rules and locked odds. Random draws never decide a win.

Ten newly accepted bets per document trigger one consultation offer; an eleventh is blocked. Refresh creates a new isolated session with exactly $1,000, zero active positions, empty recent plays and ten new plays. Prior audit records remain in IndexedDB under their previous scope, but are not resumed or credited into the new session. Each tab has its own balance and allowance. The consultation link is the approved dummy https://example.com and makes no appointment.
